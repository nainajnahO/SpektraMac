SpektraMac

Physically based analog film for your photos, on your Mac's GPU. Choose a film and a paper, and see your photo exposed, developed, printed and scanned, from the film's own spectral data.

One photo, many films.

Drag across the photo to compare the original with the film. Each render is the whole simulation: the film's exposure and development, a print on paper or the slide itself, and its scan.

Original

A darkroom, modelled.

SpektraMac ports Spektrafilm, Andrea Volpato's simulation of colour film, to Swift and Metal. Every control maps to something physical.

Films and papers

20 colour negatives, slides and motion picture stocks, from Portra, Ektar and Gold to Velvia, Kodachrome and Vision3, printed on 8 papers and print films, or viewed as film.

The camera

Exposure metered like a camera, on the whole frame or on the faces, people or objects it finds; highlight recovery; and diffusion filters from Glimmerglass to CineBloom.

The film

Halation and light scattering in the emulsion, the DIR couplers that give film its saturation and edge contrast, and grain from silver halide particles.

The print

Enlarger exposure and colour filters, preflash, paper contrast, and a burn for bright skies, all shown on a tone graph you can drag.

See before you choose

Point at a film or paper in its menu and the photo shows it at once. Click the photo for the original, at the same zoom.

Wide colour

The preview and saved photos are in Display P3, so saturated colours keep their depth on a Mac's display.

Save and reuse

Save JPEG, or 16-bit TIFF and PNG, with the edit recorded in the file; keep presets and per-film defaults; export a 3D LUT.

Opens what your Mac opens

RAW files from your camera, HEIC, JPEG, TIFF and PNG, decoded by macOS itself.

Fast enough to edit live.

The whole simulation, grain and halation included, runs on the GPU. Measured on a MacBook Pro with an M1 Pro and 16 GB of memory, against the Python Spektrafilm 0.3.4 it ports, with the same film, paper and settings.

Faithful to the reference.

The port is tested stage by stage against dumps from the Python Spektrafilm 0.3.4: every step of the pipeline, for negatives, slides and motion picture film, on a test chart and a photo. Where the Mac takes a faster route, such as Apple's blurs, the difference is measured and kept small.

Build it.

SpektraMac is free and open source. There's no download yet: build it with Xcode in a few minutes.

  1. On macOS 26.5 or newer, install Xcode 27.
  2. Clone the repository and open the project: 
    git clone https://github.com/nainajnahO/SpektraMac.git
open SpektraMac/SpektraMac.xcodeproj
  3. In the target's Signing & Capabilities, choose your own team, or Sign to Run Locally.
  4. Choose Product › Run (⌘R).
